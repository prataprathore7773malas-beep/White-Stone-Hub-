Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide imported marble?

Yes, White Stone Hub is a certified direct premium imported marble dealer. We procure high-end exotic variations straight from top-tier quarries across Italy, Turkey, Spain, and Greece, safely brought to our Kishangarh stockyard channels.

Do you deliver across India?

Absolutely. We manage logistical supply configurations providing reliable domestic heavy vehicle flatbed shipping to all states, ensuring premium stones arrive unbroken directly at your site or warehouse address lines.

Can I order custom size slabs?

Yes, utilizing our robust cutting machinery units, we can process blocks into specific structural dimensional scale sheets based fully upon structural architectural drawings.

Do you provide installation?

Yes, we have experienced team structural installation crews who deal with elite marble alignment mapping, accurate joint filling, pattern match setup, and expert mirror diamond abrasive polishing treatments.

Which marble is best for flooring?

Italian Marbles like Statuario, Michaelangelo, and Bottochino are highly preferred for ultra-luxury living spaces due to clear gloss, while premium Indian dynamic marbles are favored for massive heavy-footfall domestic usage surfaces.

Do you export marble?

Yes, as a licensed Natural Stone Exporter, we handle export documentation packing configurations utilizing robust wooden frameworks designed safely for seamless oceanic container container loading requirements.

What is your delivery time?

Standard on-shelf material catalog variations are dispatched from Kishangarh within 24 to 48 hours. Custom cut structural requests timing depends entirely upon specific lot volume metrics.
