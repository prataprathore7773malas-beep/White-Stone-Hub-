ABOUT WHITE STONE HUB

Rajasthan's Trusted Destination For Premium Marble & Granite

White Stone Hub is one of the leading marble and granite suppliers in Kishangarh, Rajasthan, offering an exclusive range of premium natural stones for residential, commercial and luxury projects. Our collection includes imported marble, Indian marble, granite, onyx, quartz and designer stones carefully selected for unmatched beauty and durability.

With more than two decades of industry experience, we are committed to delivering exceptional quality, transparent pricing and professional customer service. From elegant homes and luxury villas to hotels, offices and commercial developments, we provide complete stone solutions tailored to every project.
